About

Academic profile

Research-driven engineering with experience across academia and advanced automotive technology.

Dr. MOHAMMAD HOSSEIN BASIRI

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology. My academic and engineering background spans control systems, intelligent sensing, state estimation, robotics, connected and automated vehicles, and cyber-physical systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering (joint with Systems Design Engineering) — Systems and Controls from the University of Waterloo. During my doctoral studies, I was a member of the Real-time Embedded Software Group and the Smart Hybrid and Electric Vehicle Systems laboratory.

After completing my Ph.D., I served as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU). I subsequently worked in the automotive industry on advanced driver-assistance systems, contributing to the development of intelligent vehicle technologies.

Research

Research directions

Core areas of interest across intelligent systems, robotics, control and autonomous mobility.

01

Control Theory & Estimation

State estimation, Kalman filtering, robust inference, nonlinear systems, and control under uncertainty.

02

Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Robotic locomotion, contact detection, autonomous systems, and reliable state inference.

03

Intelligent Sensing & Sensor Fusion

Multi-sensor fusion, wireless sensing, probabilistic estimation, and intelligent sensing architectures.

04

Connected & Automated Vehicles

Vehicle control, cooperative systems, autonomous mobility, and connected vehicle technologies.

05

ADAS & Automotive Control

Advanced driver-assistance systems, vehicle motion control, parking assistance, and intelligent automotive functions.

06

Cyber-Physical Systems & Security

Secure estimation and control of networked cyber-physical systems, including vehicle platoons.
